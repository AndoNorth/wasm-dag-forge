# Design spikes

Short durable summaries of the spikes run before the epics were executed.
Spikes S3 to S5 were run on 2026-10-27, S2 on 2026-10-29 and S8 on 2026-11-01, and the results were applied to the epics.

| Spike | Question | Result | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Temporal](temporal.md) (S3, S3b) | Does the workflow design hold on Temporal with the Python SDK, and what does it need at 100,000 jobs? | Five of six thresholds pass and memory growth fails, then traced to two history caches that never evict at default sizes | Keep the workflow design, set the two cache keys, size Temporal at 1 CPU and 1 GiB |
| [Broker client](broker-client.md) (S4) | Does a thin qpid-proton wrapper give the counting retry, dead-letter reason and byte-identical replay on RabbitMQ 4.4.0? | All five checks pass, 35 of 35 assertions | AMQP 1.0 with the proton wrapper, no fallback |
| [Object store](object-store.md) (S5) | Which S3-compatible store replaces MinIO? | SeaweedFS 4.51 and RustFS 1.0.4 both pass 14 of 14 and refuse a wrong-role write | SeaweedFS 4.51 by the ordering rule, RustFS 1.0.4 as the documented alternative |
| [Footprint](S2/footprint.md) (S2) | Does the `ci` preset fit a 7.8 GiB host with its nodes at their limits, and what host does each preset need? | Fail: the shipped `ci` nodes cannot admit the sized compile and aggregate pods, and 3.6 GiB of stand-ins leave the host at its 1 GiB floor | Resize the nodes (applied in `deploy/presets/`), `ci` runs on a CI runner, `dev` on a 16 GiB VM, `soak` on a 20 GiB VM, and this laptop runs only reduced clusters |
| [State footprint](S8/state-footprint.md) (S8) | What do the real app Postgres with TimescaleDB and the object store with its admin and lifecycle worker need, and what infra node and `wdf-infra` quota do they need with Temporal, its Postgres and RabbitMQ? | Pass: app Postgres peaks at 642 MiB and the store at 533 MiB with its heap bounded, while the shipped store settings were OOM killed at 1 GiB, so the infra node is 5,120 MiB and the quota 4,772 MiB | Resize the `ci` and `dev` infra node to 5,120 MiB, set `GOMEMLIMIT` on the store, 16,252 bytes per job uncompressed and 7,001 compressed |
| [Cold start](S6/cold-start.md) (S6) | Does a first run of an 18 MB workload component fit the `ci` simulate slot of 250m and 384 MiB, and what is the guest overhead? | Pass: 3 of 3 first runs complete in 29 s at a working set of up to 326 MiB, and warm runs in 0.5 s at 70 MiB, but a first run keeps its compile memory, so a guest that holds 128 MiB is OOM killed after a cold compile and not after a warm one | Guest overhead of 128 MiB on condition that a pod warms the module cache with a separate `noop` run before a component's first task, otherwise a slot resize, `wasmtime` serial and Winch compilation do not lower the peak |
| [Heavy compile](S1b/heavy-compile.md) (S1b) | Does the workload library bundle build inside the `ci` compile slot of 1 CPU and 1 GiB, which generated bundle size is killed at that slot, and can the build step run with no network? | Fail on one part: 3 of 3 stand-in builds peak at 417 MiB in 7.7 s and build with `--network none`, but no generated bundle is OOM killed, because `componentize-py` 0.25.1 refuses a snapshot above its hostcall fuel budget with exit 1 at 2.5 MiB of source, after peaking at 929 MiB at 2.25 MiB | No slot resize, C.2 keeps only the bundle that exceeds a short `--timeout`, the compile OOM at the slot goes to the failure scenarios epic, and `unshare --user --net` is refused under the default seccomp profile, so D9 (a) always falls back |
| [Versioning](S9/versioning.md) (S9) | Does pinned versioning hold on `temporalio` 1.35.0 against the pinned dev server, do the raw deployment calls set the current version and move an open pinned workflow to a fixed build, and does a visibility count detect that a build is drained? | Pass: on server 1.32.0, a pinned workflow finishes on A while B is current, the raw calls set A then B and override a workflow from A to B through `workflow.patched`, one unversioned pool worker serves both, and the count of A is 0 at once while the server reports drained 176 s later | Keep D6, the override uses the mask path `versioning_override` and is checked with a describe, an empty mask is a silent no-op, an override schedules no task, and an override to a build that does not replay is recovered by overriding back |
| [Chart install](S10/helm-install.md) (S10) | Does the official chart `temporal` 1.8.0 with Postgres persistence, SQL visibility, one replica per service, 512 shards and the two cache keys fit Temporal's 1,024 MiB and 1 CPU on the `ci` infra node, and do the keys reach the history service? | Pass: 3,000 of 3,000 jobs finish, the four per-pod peaks sum to 502 MiB of 1,024 MiB, the mutable state cache holds at exactly 1,000, and no `memory.events` `max` counter rises, while history runs at 83 percent of its 450m and is throttled in 21 percent of periods | Deploy the chart with the starting split as the limits, the one-pod fallback of decision 20 is not needed, and the cache series is `cache_usage{cache_type="mutablestate",service_name="history",type="history"}` |
